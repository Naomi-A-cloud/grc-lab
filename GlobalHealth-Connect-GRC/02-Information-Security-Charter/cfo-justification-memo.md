# CFO Justification Memo

**To:** Marcus Thorne, Chief Financial Officer  
**From:** Information Security Governance Team  
**Subject:** Business Justification for the Information Security Charter  
**Organisation:** GlobalHealth Connect  

## Purpose

The Information Security Charter provides GHC with a formal structure for managing information security. This is important because GHC is expanding into new markets, has acquired two organisations and operates a cloud-based patient management platform. Clear security responsibilities and oversight will help reduce security risks while supporting business growth and service availability.

The Charter also supports GHC's strategic goals by protecting patient information, supporting regulatory compliance and improving accountability. Formal governance can help reduce the financial and operational impact of security incidents by ensuring that risks are identified, assigned to owners and reported to management. The Charter also provides a basis for measuring security performance and deciding where security resources should be prioritised.

## Business Value

The Information Security Charter can help GHC:

- Protect patient and business information.
- Reduce the likelihood and impact of security incidents.
- Support regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Improve accountability for security activities.
- Support reliable and secure cloud services.
- Provide management with better visibility of security risks.
- Help ensure security investments are aligned with business priorities.

## Recommendation

Management should approve and support the Information Security Charter and provide the appropriate resources needed to implement its governance requirements.
