# GlobalHealth Connect — Governance Maturity Assessment

## Assessment Purpose

This assessment evaluates the current maturity of information security governance at GlobalHealth Connect.

The assessment uses a five-level maturity scale:

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 — Ad Hoc | Activities are informal and reactive |
| 2 — Initial | Some processes exist but are inconsistent |
| 3 — Defined | Processes are documented and consistently applied |
| 4 — Managed | Processes are measured and actively managed |
| 5 — Optimising | Processes are continuously improved |

---

## Maturity Assessment

| Governance Domain | Current Evidence | Maturity | Key Gap |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Policy & Documentation | Policies are inconsistent, inherited or not current | 1 — Ad Hoc | Policies need to be reviewed, standardised and formally maintained |
| Roles & Responsibilities | Security ownership is unclear | 1 — Ad Hoc | Responsibilities and accountability need to be formally defined |
| Risk Management | Risk management is mainly reactive | 2 — Initial | A consistent risk assessment and treatment process is required |
| Metrics & Reporting | Technical metrics are not consistently translated into business impact | 2 — Initial | Metrics need business context, trends and clear escalation |
| Training & Awareness | Annual training exists but employee engagement is low | 2 — Initial | Training needs stronger engagement and effectiveness measurement |
| Compliance | Compliance activity is mainly audit-driven | 2 — Initial | Compliance should become an ongoing governance process |

---

## Overall Maturity

### Current Overall Level: 2 — Initial

GlobalHealth Connect is assessed at Level 2 — Initial overall.

Some security and compliance activities exist, but they are not yet consistently governed, measured or integrated into business processes.

The strongest immediate need is to move from reactive activities to documented and repeatable governance processes.

---

## Key Maturity Gaps

### 1. Policy & Documentation

Policies are inconsistent or outdated.

Improvement needed:

- Review existing policies.
- Remove outdated versions.
- Assign policy owners.
- Establish a formal review cycle.
- Maintain an approved policy register.

### 2. Roles & Responsibilities

Security responsibilities are not clearly defined.

Improvement needed:

- Define security ownership.
- Maintain a RACI matrix.
- Assign control owners.
- Establish escalation responsibilities.

### 3. Risk Management

Risk management is mainly reactive.

Improvement needed:

- Establish a formal risk register.
- Perform regular risk assessments.
- Assign risk owners.
- Track risk treatment actions.
- Escalate high risks.

### 4. Metrics & Reporting

Technical metrics are not consistently connected to business impact.

Improvement needed:

- Define key security metrics.
- Track trends.
- Translate technical findings into business risks.
- Establish reporting thresholds.
- Provide regular management and Board reporting.

### 5. Training & Awareness

Annual training exists, but engagement is low.

Improvement needed:

- Increase training participation.
- Introduce security awareness campaigns.
- Measure training effectiveness.
- Use phishing simulations or similar exercises.
- Track repeat failures.

### 6. Compliance

Compliance is mainly driven by audits.

Improvement needed:

- Maintain a compliance obligations register.
- Map requirements to controls.
- Track compliance continuously.
- Assign compliance owners.
- Monitor outstanding findings.

---

## Target State

The target is to move GlobalHealth Connect toward Level 3 — Defined within the first 12–18 months.

At Level 3:

- Security policies are documented and approved.
- Responsibilities are clearly assigned.
- Risk management is repeatable.
- Security metrics are regularly reported.
- Training effectiveness is measured.
- Compliance activities are continuously tracked.

---

## Assessment Conclusion

GlobalHealth Connect has established some security and compliance activities, but governance remains largely reactive.

The organisation should prioritise formalising policies, assigning accountability, improving risk management, strengthening reporting and moving compliance from an audit-driven activity to a continuous governance process.
