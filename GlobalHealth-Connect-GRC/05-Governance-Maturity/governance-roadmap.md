# GlobalHealth Connect — 12–18 Month Governance Improvement Roadmap

## Roadmap Objective

The objective of this roadmap is to move GlobalHealth Connect from Level 2 — Initial toward Level 3 — Defined information security governance maturity.

The roadmap focuses on creating documented, repeatable and clearly owned governance processes.

---

## Phase 1 — Months 1–3

### Initiative 1: Policy and Documentation Standardisation

Objective:  
Create a consistent and controlled information security policy structure.

Activities:

- Review existing security policies.
- Identify outdated or duplicated policies.
- Assign policy owners.
- Establish a policy approval process.
- Create a policy review schedule.

Measurable Outcome:

100% of critical security policies have an assigned owner, approval status and review date.

---

### Initiative 2: Define Security Roles and Accountability

Objective: 
Establish clear ownership for information security responsibilities.

Activities:

- Review the existing RACI matrix.
- Assign security and control owners.
- Define escalation responsibilities.
- Communicate responsibilities to relevant teams.

Measurable Outcome:

100% of priority security activities have documented owners and escalation paths.

---

## Phase 2 — Months 4–6

### Initiative 3: Formalise Risk Management

Objective:  
Move from reactive risk management to a consistent risk assessment and treatment process.

Activities:

- Create an information security risk register.
- Define risk assessment criteria.
- Assign risk owners.
- Document risk treatment actions.
- Establish review and escalation requirements.

Measurable Outcome:

100% of high and critical security risks have an assigned owner and documented treatment plan.

---

### Initiative 4: Improve Security Metrics and Reporting

Objective:
Translate technical security information into business-focused reporting.

Activities:

- Define key security metrics.
- Establish reporting thresholds.
- Track monthly trends.
- Link security metrics to business impact.
- Provide regular reports to the Security Steering Committee.

Measurable Outcome:

Monthly security reporting is established with documented trends, business impact and recommended actions.

---

## Phase 3 — Months 7–12

5. Strengthen Training, Awareness & Continuous Compliance

Objective:
Improve employee security awareness while making compliance a continuous governance activity.

Activities:
- Increase security training participation.
- Run regular security awareness campaigns.
- Conduct phishing simulations.
- Track repeated training failures.
- Create a compliance obligations register.
- Map priority requirements to security controls and owners.
- Track compliance gaps and remediation actions.
- Report important compliance issues to management.

Measurable Outcome:
- Security training completion reaches at least 90%.
- Phishing simulation results are tracked and reviewed.
- 100% of priority compliance requirements are mapped to responsible owners and controls.
- Compliance gaps have documented remediation actions.

---

## Phase 4 — Months 13–18

### Governance Integration and Continuous Improvement

Objective:
Embed governance processes into normal business operations.

Activities:

- Review governance performance.
- Measure maturity progress.
- Review unresolved risks.
- Evaluate policy effectiveness.
- Review security metrics and trends.
- Identify further improvement opportunities.

Measurable Outcome:

GlobalHealth Connect demonstrates consistent and documented governance processes across all six assessment domains.

---

## Roadmap Summary

| Period | Priority | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1–3 | Policies & accountability | Clear ownership and controlled documentation |
| Months 4–6 | Risk & reporting | Repeatable risk management and business-focused reporting |
| Months 7–12 | Training & compliance | Measurable awareness and continuous compliance |
| Months 13–18 | Governance integration | Consistent governance and continuous improvement |

---

## Success Measures

The roadmap will be considered successful when:

- Critical policies have documented owners and review dates.
- Security responsibilities are clearly assigned.
- High and critical risks have owners and treatment plans.
- Security metrics are regularly reported to management.
- Training effectiveness is measured.
- Compliance obligations are continuously monitored.
- Governance activities are documented and repeatable.

---

## Target Maturity

Current: Level 2 — Initial

Target: Level 3 — Defined

The target is to establish consistent, documented and repeatable information security governance processes within 12–18 months.
