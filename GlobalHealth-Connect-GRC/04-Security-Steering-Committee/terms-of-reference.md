# GlobalHealth Connect — Security Steering Committee Terms of Reference

## 1. Purpose

The Security Steering Committee (SSC) provides governance and oversight for information security at GlobalHealth Connect.

The committee ensures that security decisions support business objectives, manage information security risks and protect sensitive information.

---

## 2. Objectives

The SSC will:

- Review significant information security risks.
- Provide direction on security priorities.
- Review security policies and major changes.
- Monitor security performance and key metrics.
- Support incident response governance.
- Resolve security issues that require cross-functional decisions.
- Ensure security activities support business and regulatory requirements.

---

## 4. Authority

The SSC is authorised to:

- Review and recommend information security policies.
- Review significant security risks.
- Request corrective actions from responsible teams.
- Escalate unresolved high-risk issues to the CEO.
- Recommend security investments and priorities.
- Monitor progress against agreed security actions.

Major decisions with significant financial, legal or strategic impact will be escalated to executive management or the Board as appropriate.

---

## 5. Key Responsibilities

### Governance

The committee provides oversight of information security governance and ensures that responsibilities are clearly assigned.

### Risk Management

The committee reviews significant information security risks and ensures that appropriate treatment actions are identified and tracked.

### Policy Oversight

The committee reviews proposed security policies and major policy changes before approval through the appropriate governance authority.

### Incident Governance

The committee reviews significant incidents, response actions and lessons learned.

### Performance Monitoring

The committee reviews security metrics, trends and outstanding remediation activities.

### Compliance

The committee monitors significant compliance requirements and security-related findings.

---

## 6. Decision-Making

The SSC will use a risk-based approach when making security decisions.

Decisions should consider:

- Security risk
- Business impact
- Cost
- User impact
- Regulatory requirements
- Technical feasibility
- Availability and operational requirements

Where different stakeholders have competing priorities, the committee will assess the risks and business impact before reaching a decision.

Unresolved high-risk issues will be escalated to the CEO.

---

## 7. Meeting Frequency

The SSC will meet at least monthly.

Additional meetings may be called when a significant security incident or urgent security decision requires committee attention.

---

## 8. Meeting Records

The committee secretary or assigned member will maintain:

- Meeting agenda
- Attendance
- Key decisions
- Assigned actions
- Action owners
- Target completion dates
- Escalated issues

Outstanding actions will be reviewed at subsequent meetings.

---

## 9. Reporting

The SSC will provide relevant security updates to executive management and the Board.

Reports may include:

- Major security risks
- Security incidents
- Vulnerability status
- Compliance issues
- Security metrics
- Outstanding remediation actions
- Decisions requiring executive attention

---

## 10. Review of Terms of Reference

These Terms of Reference will be reviewed at least annually or when there are significant changes to the organisation, security risks or governance structure.

---

## 11. Expected Outcome

The SSC provides a formal governance mechanism for making security decisions.

It ensures that security is not managed only as a technical responsibility but is considered as an organisational risk and business priority.
