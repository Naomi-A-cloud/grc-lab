# GlobalHealth Connect Security Metrics

## Reporting Period

September 2025 – February 2026

## Purpose

These metrics provide management and the Board with visibility into GlobalHealth Connect's security posture and key security trends.

## Security Metrics

| Month | Phishing Attempts | Malware Incidents | Critical Vulnerabilities | Critical Vulnerabilities Patched | Patching Rate | Security Training Completion | High-Risk Incidents |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| October | 180 | 30 | 12 | 20 | 60% | 50% | 3 |
| November | 210 | 35 | 15 | 25 | 60% | 55% | 4 |

## Key Trends

### Phishing

Phishing attempts increased from 150 in September to 320 in February.

This indicates increasing exposure to phishing threats and the need for stronger preventive and awareness controls.

### Malware

Malware incidents increased from 25 to 50 during the reporting period.

This indicates that the organisation continues to face increasing malicious activity.

### Critical Vulnerabilities

Critical vulnerabilities increased from 10 to 22.

Although more vulnerabilities were patched, the overall number of critical vulnerabilities increased.

### Patching

The patching rate decreased from 67% to 55%.

This is a negative trend because a lower patching rate may increase exposure to known vulnerabilities.

### Security Training

Training completion increased from 45% to 70%.

This is a positive trend and shows improvement in employee security awareness.

### High-Risk Incidents

High-risk incidents increased from 2 to 7.

This is a significant negative trend that requires management attention.

## Overall Security Posture

The overall security posture is assessed as:

**RED**

The main reasons are the increase in phishing, malware and high-risk incidents combined with declining patching performance.

Although security training improved, the improvement has not yet resulted in a reduction in the other key security indicators.

## Priority Risks

### Risk 1: Increasing High-Risk Security Incidents

High-risk incidents increased from 2 to 7 during the reporting period.

**Recommendation:**

Strengthen incident detection, response and preventive controls. Track significant incidents and corrective actions through the Security Steering Committee.

### Risk 2: Declining Patching Performance

Patching performance declined from 67% to 55%.

**Recommendation:**

Establish vulnerability remediation targets based on severity and monitor overdue critical vulnerabilities through management reporting.

## Metric Selection Rationale

The selected metrics provide a balanced view of the security posture.

Phishing and malware show external and malicious activity. Critical vulnerabilities and patching show the organisation's ability to address technical weaknesses. Security training measures employee awareness. High-risk incidents show the actual impact of security events.

Together, these metrics help management understand both security performance and areas requiring action.
