# NexusTech Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes acceptable use requirements for NexusTech information systems, devices, networks and data.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers and other authorised users of NexusTech systems.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Users must:

- Protect company information from unauthorised access.
- Use company systems for legitimate business purposes.
- Keep authentication credentials confidential.
- Report suspected security incidents promptly.
- Follow applicable security policies and procedures.
- Use company devices and systems responsibly.

## 4. Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to systems.
- Share passwords or authentication credentials.
- Install unauthorised software.
- Use company systems for illegal activities.
- Circumvent security controls.
- Access information without a legitimate business need.
- Introduce malicious or unauthorised content into company systems.

## 5. Monitoring

NexusTech may monitor the use of organisational systems in accordance with applicable laws, policies and contractual requirements.

## 6. Violations

Violations may result in investigation and appropriate disciplinary or corrective action in accordance with organisational procedures.

## 7. Review

This policy should be reviewed at least annually or when significant organisational, technological or regulatory changes occur.
