# Policy Review and Maintenance Memo

## Purpose

NexusTech maintains its security policies to ensure they remain relevant, effective and aligned with organisational requirements.

## Review Triggers

Policies should be reviewed:

- At least annually.
- Following significant security incidents.
- Following major technology changes.
- Following significant organisational changes.
- When relevant legal or regulatory requirements change.
- When control weaknesses are identified.

## Review Process

1. Policy owner initiates the review.
2. Relevant stakeholders assess the policy.
3. Required changes are identified.
4. Changes are documented.
5. Appropriate management approval is obtained.
6. The updated policy is published.
7. Users are informed of significant changes.
8. Previous versions are retained according to document-control requirements.

## Accountability

Each policy should have an assigned owner responsible for maintaining the document and coordinating periodic review.

## Evidence

Examples include:

- Policy review records
- Approval records
- Version history
- Policy acknowledgement records
- Communication records
